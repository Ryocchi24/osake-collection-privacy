01Information we handle

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in. The main information the App handles is as follows.

Your drink entriesOn your device only

Drink names, ratings, tasting notes, notes, dates, places, how you had it, who you drank with, stories you heard at a bar, your wishlist, your profile name, and similar entries are stored only on your device. They are never sent to a server run by the developer or anyone else. The only exception is the paid “AI Bartender”: when you use an AI feature after agreeing to it, part of this information needed to create the answer is sent (see 4. AI Bartender).

PhotosOn your device only

Photos you take with the camera, choose from your photo library, or paste are stored only on your device. Background removal and label text recognition are also done entirely on your device and are not sent anywhere. The only exception is when AI Bartender’s AI camera identifies a drink, in which case the photo you took is sent (see 4. AI Bartender). Camera and photo access is used only when you take or choose a photo for an entry.

LocationOnly when you tap

Only when you tap “Nearby places” while logging a place, your current location is used to find nearby bars and restaurants. The search is done with Apple’s map service (MapKit), and your current location and search words are sent to Apple. The developer never collects or stores your location.

Advertising identifier (IDFA)With your permission

Only if you allow it through iOS App Tracking Transparency, the advertising identifier is used by the ad SDK (Google AdMob) to serve ads. If you don’t allow it, every feature of the App still works normally.

App usageNot personally identifying

To improve the App, we use Google’s Firebase Analytics to measure how often features are used (for example, how many entries were logged, wishlist additions, whether the tutorial was completed, whether a purchase was completed, and whether AI Bartender features were used). What is sent is the type of action and small numbers, an app-specific identifier for your device, your device model, your approximate region, and similar data. The contents of your entries, such as drink names, notes, photos, and who you drank with, are never sent. Google handles this data under Google’s privacy policy.

Purchase information

In-app purchases are processed through Apple’s App Store payment system. The developer never receives or stores payment details such as credit card numbers. If you use AI Bartender, the purchase record issued by the App Store (such as the transaction ID) is sent to the developer’s server to confirm your membership and count your uses. It does not include your name, email address, or Apple Account.

02Ads (AdMob)

To cover running costs, the App uses Google AdMob, provided by Google. AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier, approximate region, and similar information to serve ads and measure their performance.

The App shows the following ads. Every ad is labeled as an ad.

04AI Bartender

Answers from the paid “AI Bartender” are created by Claude, an AI provided by Anthropic (USA). Before you use AI Bartender for the first time, the App tells you what will be sent and where, and only if you agree, the following information is sent to Anthropic through the developer’s server (running on Cloudflare’s services), only when you use an AI feature. This includes when you take a photo with the AI camera (if the label can’t be read, the bottle is identified automatically) and when your monthly Review “title” arrives at the end of the month.

Names of drinks you’ve logged and drinks on your wishlistWhen picking recommendations (so the same drinks aren’t suggested)

Names of people you drank with, place names, notes, location, and your name or email address are never sent.

  • Anthropic states that it does not use information sent through its API to train its AI. Anthropic handles this information under its own privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • The developer’s server stores your purchase record (transaction ID) and monthly use count to count your uses, parts of the answers it created (to reuse notes on the same drink), and any “Something wrong?” reports. Notes on drinks in the drink library may be reused for other users, in a form that doesn’t include anyone’s entries. Photos are used only to create the answer and are not stored on the developer’s server.
  • You can withdraw your consent anytime in the “AI Bartender” section of My Page. Once withdrawn, nothing is sent until you agree again.

05Network connections

The App does not send the contents of your entries anywhere. It does connect to the network in the following cases.

  • Drink library updates: The App checks GitHub’s service (raw.githubusercontent.com) for a new version of the drink library used for search, and downloads it if one is available. No entry contents or personally identifying information are sent (by the nature of the connection, your IP address reaches GitHub).
  • Place search: When you type a place name to search, or tap “Nearby places,” your search words (and current location) are sent to Apple’s map service.
  • Ads: Ads served by Google AdMob, as described above.
  • AI Bartender: When a member asks after agreeing, the information described above is sent, and the remaining uses for the month are checked.
  • Usage measurement: Feature usage sent through Firebase Analytics, as described above.

06Links to other sites

In the Japanese App Store version only, “Buy this drink” on a drink’s detail screen opens search results on Rakuten Ichiba or Amazon.co.jp. These links include referral codes for the Rakuten Affiliate and Amazon Associates programs, and the developer may earn a referral fee if a product is purchased through them. As an Amazon Associate, the App earns from qualifying purchases. These links are not shown outside Japan.

“Search online” on the photo screen opens Google Image Search in your browser. Information on linked sites is handled under each company’s privacy policy.

07Where data is stored

Your entries and photos are stored only in iOS local storage on your device (for information stored on the developer’s server for AI Bartender, see “4. AI Bartender”). The App does not offer backups to an external server, so deleting the App deletes your entries and photos. Before switching devices, please use an iPhone backup.

You can export your entries as a CSV file with “Export entries” on My Page. You choose where the exported file is saved or shared.

08Sharing with third parties

Except for Google AdMob (ads), Google Firebase Analytics (usage measurement), Apple’s StoreKit (purchases) and MapKit (place search), GitHub (drink library delivery), and Anthropic and Cloudflare (AI Bartender) as described above, the App never provides or sells collected information to third parties.

09Age requirement

The App is for logging alcoholic drinks. You must be of legal drinking age in your country or region to use it (20 or older in Japan). The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10Your choices

  • You can turn off tracking permission for all apps, including this one, anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can change camera, photo, and location access anytime in iOS Settings → Drink Collection.
  • You can delete drinks and entries in the App anytime. Deleting the App deletes all entries and photos stored on your device.
  • To stop the ads in your list and Review, purchase “No Ads + All Wallpapers” in the App or subscribe to AI Bartender.
  • You can withdraw your consent to send information to AI Bartender anytime in the “AI Bartender” section of My Page.

11Changes to this policy

This policy may change without notice due to changes in law or new features in the App. If there is an important change, we will announce it on this page.
